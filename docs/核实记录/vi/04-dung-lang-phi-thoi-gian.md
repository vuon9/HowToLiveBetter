## Mục 1, 2: Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006)
- Mở: <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1> (302 → linkinghub.elsevier.com, trang nội dung chỉ trả về Redirecting); <https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/DOI:10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1>; <https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1>
- Khớp tiêu đề: Đúng. Crossref: Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement: A Meta-analysis of Effects and Processes, Advances in Experimental Social Psychology vol. 38, pp. 69–119, 2006
- Nguồn số liệu (Nguyên văn tóm tắt trên Semantic Scholar): "Findings from 94 independent tests showed that implementation intentions had a positive effect of medium-to-large magnitude (d = .65) on goal attainment. Implementation intentions were effective in promoting the initiation of goal striving, the shielding of ongoing goal pursuit from unwanted influences, disengagement from failing courses of action, and conservation of capability for future goal striving."

## Mục 3: Arkes & Blumer (1985)
- Mở: <https://doi.org/10.1016/0749-5978(85)90049-4> (→ linkinghub chỉ trả về Redirecting); <https://api.openalex.org/works/doi:10.1016/0749-5978(85)90049-4> (tiêu đề, tác giả, tạp chí, năm đã xác nhận, phần tóm tắt để trống); <https://www.sciencedirect.com> (403/mã xác thực); <https://r.jina.ai/https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e4564b88ca2349962a707b76be4c75076ad6bd43> (tóm tắt); <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2796842/> (trang trích dẫn 35, 124–140)
- Khớp tiêu đề: Đúng. The psychology of sunk cost, Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes, 1985
- Nguồn số liệu: Nguyên văn tóm tắt "In a field study, customers who had initially paid more for a season subscription to a theater series attended more plays during the next 6 months, presumably because of their higher sunk cost in the season tickets". Số buổi cụ thể của từng nhóm (ví dụ 4,11 buổi) **chưa xác nhận**, trong mục đánh dấu là chờ xác minh, không ghi số liệu.

## Mục 3: Roth, Robbert & Straus (2015)
- Mở: <https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/DOI:10.1007/s40685-014-0014-8>; <https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1007/s40685-014-0014-8>

## Mục 4, 8: Buehler, Griffin & Ross (1994)
- Mở: <https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.67.3.366> (→ psycnet, 403); <https://api.openalex.org/works/doi:10.1037/0022-3514.67.3.366> (tiêu đề, tạp chí đã xác nhận, có tóm tắt); PDF toàn văn <https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/biases/67_J_Personality_and_Social_Psychology_366,_1994.pdf> (trích xuất pdftotext cục bộ)
- Khớp tiêu đề: Đúng. Trang đầu PDF: Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 1994, Vol. 67, No. 3, 366-381
- Nguồn số liệu (Nội dung Study 1): "respondents predicted, on average, that they would finish in 33.9 days, but they actually took 55.5 days … Fewer than one third of the respondents (29.7%) finished in the time they reported as their most accurate prediction."
- Nguồn số liệu (Nội dung Study 4): "…in the recall-relevant condition (60.0%) than in the recall and control conditions (38.1% and 29.3%, respectively)"; "Note, n = 41, 42, and 40 in the control, recall, and recall-relevant conditions"; Tóm tắt: "In Study 4, the bias was eliminated for participants instructed to connect past experiences with their predictions."
- Nguồn số liệu (Nội dung Study 2, mục 8): "A subset of the subjects (n = 62) reported having external deadlines … a majority of these subjects (80.6%) finished the projects in time to meet their deadlines … only 38.7% of these subjects finished in the predicted time … their reported completion times were strongly associated with the deadlines (r = .82, p < .001)"; predictions và deadline chỉ đạt r = .23.

## Mục 13: Stutzer & Frey (2008)
