## 期刊论文
### Sasson 2010（mục 1）
- DOI: 10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.109.889576（doi.org 302 → ahajournals.org 403）
- Đã mở: PubMed 20123673 và giao diện Europe PMC, phần tóm tắt khớp nhau.
- Nguyên văn: 「79 studies involving 142,740 patients」「pooled survival to hospital discharge was 7.6%」「witnessed by a bystander (6.4% to 13.5%) … who received bystander CPR (3.9% to 16.1%)」「Although 53% (95% CI, 45.0% to 59.9%) of events were witnessed by a bystander, only 32% (95% CI, 26.7% to 37.8%) received bystander CPR」

### Zheng 2023 BASIC-OHCA（mục 1）
- Tra tiêu đề trên Europe PMC có kết quả: Zheng J, Lv C, Zheng W et al. Incidence, process of care, and outcomes of out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in China: a prospective study of the BASIC-OHCA registry. The Lancet Public Health, 2023. DOI 10.1016/S2468-2667(23)00173-1
- Nguyên văn: 「7121 (20.3%) received bystander cardiopulmonary resuscitation」「441 (1.2%) of 38,227 survived」（xuất viện hoặc 30 ngày）
- Cùng đợt tra còn có Xie X et al. 2023 Circ Cardiovasc Qual Outcomes (DOI 10.1161/circoutcomes.121.008856): tỉ lệ sống khi xuất viện của các ca do EMS xử lý là 1,15%, không trích trong bài.

### Teixeira 2018（mục 2）
- DOI 10.1016/j.jamcollsurg.2017.12.016 mà đề bài đưa, qua Europe PMC kiểm chứng lại trỏ sang một bài về ung thư dạ dày khác (Woo Y et al.), **đã bỏ**.
- Dùng tiêu đề tra Europe PMC ra DOI đúng: 10.1016/j.jamcollsurg.2018.01.047. Teixeira PGR, Brown CVR, Emigh B et al., Texas Tourniquet Study Group. Civilian Prehospital Tourniquet Use Is Associated with Improved Survival in Patients with Peripheral Vascular Injury. J Am Coll Surg 2018.
- Nguyên văn: 「11 Level I trauma centers (January 2011 through December 2016)」「During 6 years, 1,026 patients with peripheral vascular injuries were admitted」「Prehospital tourniquets were used in 181 (17.6%) patients」「Mortality was 5.2% in the non-tourniquet group compared with 3.9% in the tourniquet group」「the use of tourniquets was found to be independently associated with survival (adjusted odds ratio 5.86; 95% CI 1.41 to 24.47; adjusted p = 0.015)」。Bản nháp đầu từng viết theo trí nhớ thành 3,4% so với 8,9%, sau khi đối chiếu lại phần tóm tắt lần hai thì sửa lại.

### Kragh 2009（mục 2）
- DOI: 10.1097/SLA.0b013e31818842ba（doi.org 302 → ovid.com 402）
- Đã mở: giao diện Europe PMC. Kragh JF Jr et al. Survival with emergency tourniquet use to stop bleeding in major limb trauma. Ann Surg 2009.
- Nguyên văn: 232 patients, 309 limbs; 「Tourniquet use when shock was absent was strongly associated with survival (90% vs. 10%; P < 0.001)」; 194 người được dùng trước khi tới bệnh viện có tỉ lệ tử vong 11%, 38 người chỉ được dùng ở phòng cấp cứu là 24%; 5 người cần dùng mà không dùng có tỉ lệ sống 0% so với 87%; 「no amputations resulted from tourniquet use alone」, liệt thần kinh tạm thời 4 ca.

### FEMA Ready.gov Home Fires (mục 7)
- <https://www.ready.gov/home-fires> đã mở.
- Nguyên văn: "Drop down to the floor and crawl low, under any smoke to your exit" "Before opening a door, feel the doorknob and door. If either is hot, or if there is smoke coming around the door, leave the door closed and use your second way out" "If you open a door, open it slowly. Be ready to shut it quickly if heavy smoke or fire is present" "stop, drop and roll" "If you can't get out, close the door and cover vents and cracks around doors with cloth or tape to keep smoke out. Call 9-1-1"
- Trang không có nguyên văn "không đi thang máy" và "ra ngoài rồi đừng ngoái lại", hai câu này được nêu trong ghi chú phần chính như kiến thức thông thường.

### FEMA Ready.gov Earthquakes (mục 13)
- <https://www.ready.gov/earthquakes> đã mở.
- Nguyên văn: "Drop where you are onto hands and knees" "Cover your head and neck with one arm and hand" "Hold until the shaking stops" "Turn face down and cover your head and neck with a pillow" "Stay there. Move to an open area away from buildings, trees, streetlights and power lines" "Pull over and stop. Set your parking brake" "If you are inside, stay and do not run outside; avoid doorways" "Expect aftershocks" "Send a text or bang on a pipe or wall. Cover your mouth with your shirt for protection and instead of shouting, use a whistle"; tránh thang máy trong tòa nhà hư hại, không vào tòa nhà hư hại.

### NWS Heat Related Illnesses (mục 6)
- <https://www.weather.gov/safety/heat-illness> đã mở.
- Nguyên văn: sốc nhiệt "body temperature above 103°F" "Call 911 or get the victim to a hospital immediately. Heat stroke is a severe medical emergency", chỗ mát, dùng khăn ướt nước mát để hạ nhiệt, "Do not provide fluids"; kiệt sức vì nóng "Loosen clothing. Apply cool, wet cloths or have person sit in a cool bath", uống từng ngụm nhỏ, "Seek immediate medical attention if the person vomits, symptoms worsen or last longer than 1 hour"

### NWS During Extreme Cold (mục 11)
- <https://www.weather.gov/safety/cold-during> đã mở (/safety/cold-hypothermia 404).
- Nguyên văn: "If your temperature is 95°F or less, you feel cold and sluggish, or you are having trouble thinking clearly, see a doctor immediately" "Get medical attention immediately. Move the victim inside to a heated location and begin warming the center of the body first" "If the person is unconscious, administer CPR", đồ uống nóng, rượu "reduces shivering"; tê cóng "Do not use hot water or radiant heat such as a fireplace"

### MedlinePlus Choking (mục 9)
- <https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000047.htm> đã mở.
- Nguyên văn: "Are you choking?
